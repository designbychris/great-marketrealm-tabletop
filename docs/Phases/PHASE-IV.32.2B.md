# Phase IV.32.2B — Pippin Demands a Bigger Office

The Pixel Chisel exposed a layout truth: the Keeper's Atlas and Bestiary are working spaces, not narrow utility pop-outs, and The Gathering needs enough room for readable identity, role, actions and vitality.

This phase is presentation-only. It does not alter Scene authority, Bestiary deployment, party membership, HP authority, Fog, Vision, lighting, token state or encounter behaviour.

## Keeper workspace

- Atlas and Bestiary share one responsive desktop drawer width.
- Their registers use comfortable auto-fitting card columns and collapse to one column on narrow screens.
- Atlas cards gain larger thumbnails and less aggressive title wrapping.
- Bestiary records inherit the same breathing room without introducing horizontal scrolling.
- Pippin's Field Note uses the larger drawer to present the canonical pixel portrait at a useful character scale.

## Adventurers demand elbow room

- The right-hand Gathering rail receives a deliberate desktop width while the battlefield remains the flexible main workspace.
- Member cards separate portrait, identity, role, table action and HP into stable grid positions.
- Portraits are enlarged and squared into the pixel vocabulary.
- HP occupies a full-width row rather than competing with the player name and Remove action.
- The Fellowship Ribbon becomes a compact eight-slot pixel grid.
- Existing single-column behaviour remains below the established Tabletop breakpoint.

## Responsive contract

Pippin receives a larger office, not horizontal scrolling. Keeper drawers remain viewport-capped and both Keeper registers collapse to a single column on small screens.
