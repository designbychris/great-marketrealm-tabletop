# Phase IV.32.2C.1 — The Keeper Tidies the Desk

A browser-feedback refinement after **IV.32.2C — The Keeper Rearranges the Furniture**.

This pass keeps the pixel presentation compact without changing Tabletop authority or gameplay behaviour:

- the Bestiary workspace opens to the same top edge as the Atlas while its rail tab remains stacked beneath Atlas;
- live Gathering refreshes preserve the character-seat portrait (or `P` placeholder) instead of reverting to the old PLAYER badge;
- the management action receives more breathing room below the seat identity;
- Fellowship Ribbon swatches regain their actual fellowship colours after the global pixel button skin;
- the old Cartographer's Lens help strip is removed;
- Fog, Lantern Rack, Sight Beyond the Door, Cartography/Forge tools and Grid Calibration live behind one compact **Dungeon Master Controls** disclosure;
- the Dungeon Master's optional Companion character picker remains available behind a compact **Choose a Test Adventurer** disclosure.

The pass is presentation and information architecture only. Scene, Fog, LOS, lighting, encounter, membership, Companion selection and token authority are unchanged.
