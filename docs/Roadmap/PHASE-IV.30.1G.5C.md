# Phase IV.30.1G.5C — Semantic Continuity & Region Topology

## The Wall Remembers Where It Was Going

G.5C teaches Hybrid Judgement to reason about an ordered boundary as a connected piece of dungeon topology rather than judging every short span in isolation.

Living Contour paths now carry conservative semantic topology evidence. Hybrid measures continuity and repeated playable-side asymmetry along the path. A locally weak straight span can therefore survive overlap trimming when it belongs to a coherent connected playable boundary, instead of being discarded merely because Structural tracing happens to occupy the same grid-aligned area.

Door and threshold evidence remains a hard semantic interruption. Region continuity is never permission to bridge a classified opening. Isolated grid lines likewise gain no authority from this phase: topology strengthens an already ordered Living Contour chain; it does not manufacture a chain from grid alignment.

This phase is intentionally review-first. It adds evidence metadata to Hybrid suggestions and preserves the existing G.4/G.5 safety contracts, partial-contour uncertainty, structural artwork corroboration, and the 200-object review budget.
