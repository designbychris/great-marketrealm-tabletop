# Phase IV.30.1G.5D — Contour Chain Certification

## The Boundary Earns Trust Together

G.5D changes Hybrid Judgement from local-span confidence to whole-chain certification. A long ordered Living Contour path can now retain locally weak sections when its distributed evidence identifies it as a coherent boundary of the same playable region.

### Behaviour

- Hybrid considers both Connected Floor and standalone Living Contour evidence instead of allowing a sparse connectivity pass to exclude a stronger standalone cave perimeter.
- Whole chains earn certification from path scale, semantic wall evidence, playable-region topology and distributed continuity.
- Certified chains survive local Structural overlap trimming; the structural reader can corroborate them without fragmenting them.
- Small interior marks remain local evidence rather than inheriting chain authority.
- Door and threshold protection remains authoritative. Certification never heals a protected opening.
- Existing G.5B structural corroboration remains in force, so the calibrated graph-paper grid still cannot become architecture merely through alignment.

### Visual acceptance target

On the Dungeon From Hell, Judgement — Hybrid should retain materially more of the legitimate cave perimeter already visible in Living Contour while keeping grid-derived square networks suppressed.
