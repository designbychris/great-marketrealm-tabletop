# Phase IV.30.2B.2 — Pippin Learns to Colour Inside the Lines

Outdoor rules geometry no longer has to double as presentation geometry. Forest and Village Forge barriers remain authoritative for LOS/Fog and remain selectable for Keeper inspection, but Forge-owned environmental barriers are visually quiet at rest. The generated SVG carries the visible scene instead.

This pass also deepens the deterministic outdoor vocabulary: tree canopies gain layered crowns, trunks and roots; rocks become irregular boulder groups; fallen logs gain bark and cut ends; Village buildings gain full roof planes, ridges, doors, windows and selected chimneys; wells and fenced gardens receive recognisable top-down details; and forest trails gain a two-stroke track treatment.

The invariant remains: Scene Type owns topology, Theme owns presentation, and Tabletop Vision/Fog consumes the authoritative geometry independently of decorative SVG detail.
