# Phase IV.28B.1 — The Keeper Clears the Shelves

The Keeper may remove an obsolete inactive Scene from the Atlas. The live Scene and the final remaining Scene are protected. Removal clears Scene-owned tokens, Fog, vision barriers, footsteps, carried/dropped/magical lights, encounters and encounter battle events, plus token-owned vitality, death saves, conditions, arsenals and combat/damage profiles. Table membership and the table-wide Chronicle are retained.

Deletion is Dungeon-Master-only and requires a named browser confirmation before the authoritative AJAX action is sent.
