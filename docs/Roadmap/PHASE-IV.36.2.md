# Phase IV.36.2 — Something Else Lives Down Here

The Dungeon Forge can now optionally populate ordinary dungeon rooms with a sparse, deterministic selection of canonical Bestiary creatures. Population is opt-in, never occupies the specialised Boss Lair, excludes the chosen boss definition, respects creature size against room dimensions, and creates hidden combat-ready tokens.

These inhabitants are preparation state, not automatic encounters. They become visible only when the Keeper deliberately includes their exact token in the existing Encounter flow. This extends the IV.36.1 boundary rather than creating a second combat system. Forest and Village population remain future ecology work.
