# Phase IV.35.9 — Convert to Mimic

Any `mimic_capable` Scene Object may be secretly bound by the Keeper to a real
Bestiary creature definition. Conversion preserves the original furnishing,
sprite and tactical geometry and stores an unrevealed `state.mimic` record.
Players continue to see the innocent furnishing; the Bestiary identity remains
Keeper information. Conversion may be removed without deleting the object.
