# Phase IV.33.2 — The Keeper Opens the Doors

The Keeper's Campaign Shelf now doubles as the front door to each campaign's Gathering.

## Delivered

- Each owned campaign card projects its persistent Table roster.
- A Keeper can expand **Manage Players** without entering the battlefield.
- Existing WordPress users can be summoned by username, email, or user ID.
- Invited and active seats are visible with role and membership status.
- Player seats can be removed from the Campaign Shelf.
- Summons and removals deliberately reuse the existing nonce-protected Gathering endpoints and active-DM authority; IV.33.2 creates no parallel membership system.
- The existing in-Table Gathering controls remain available.

## Browser certification

From the bare Tabletop URL as a Dungeon Master, expand **Manage Players** on a campaign, send a Summons, confirm the seat appears as Invited, accept it as that player, and confirm the Shelf subsequently shows Active. Remove the player and confirm the seat becomes Left and can no longer enter the Table without a fresh Summons.
