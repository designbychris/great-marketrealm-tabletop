# Phase IV.36.6 — The Forge Tells a Story

The Dungeon Forge now turns its semantic preparation into a deterministic Keeper-facing adventure brief.

- Dungeon-only, opt-in **Tell a story** on both Forge surfaces.
- Story derives from the same entrance, rooms, occupants, secrets, traps, Boss Lair and treasure already prepared by the Forge.
- Produces a title, hook, ordered adventure beats, semantic summary and Pippin Keeper note.
- Story is preparation metadata: it never crosses the Player HTML or live AJAX boundary.
- No parallel quest engine is introduced; this phase narrates canonical Forge state rather than duplicating it.
