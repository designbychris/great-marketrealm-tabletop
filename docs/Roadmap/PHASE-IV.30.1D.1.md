# Phase IV.30.1D.1 — The Connected Dungeon

## Playable-Floor Connectivity & Region Graph

The Cartographer's Judgement can already see constructed and organic boundaries locally. This corrective teaches the hybrid reader to reason about the playable floor as connected dungeon regions before producing final review linework.

### Contract

- The calibrated gameplay grid remains authoritative for scale; the connectivity graph uses the temporary fine analysis mesh.
- Hybrid Judgement may heal a one-cell low-confidence ink/grid seam only when playable floor exists directly on opposite sides.
- A seam is never healed merely because two regions are nearby, and diagonal/corner proximity alone does not create a connection.
- Connected components smaller than the meaningful-floor threshold are treated as likely hatch gaps, handwriting or decoration rather than rooms.
- Standalone Living Contour keeps its established cave behaviour; connected-floor healing is hybrid-only.
- Structural tracing still supplies genuine constructed walls after connectivity healing, so the graph does not replace architectural evidence.
- Openings remain review-first: Pippin does not bridge arbitrary gaps to make the map look tidy.
- Nothing is saved automatically. The Keeper still reviews and explicitly applies selected linework.
- The hostile misaligned-grid benchmark remains reserved for later grid-registration work.

### Browser benchmark

Use the mixed cave-and-constructed dungeon as the Connected Dungeon benchmark. Success means rooms and corridors read as one coherent traversable network while cave contours remain wall-hugging, structural rooms stay crisp, and isolated decorative white pockets do not become false chambers.
