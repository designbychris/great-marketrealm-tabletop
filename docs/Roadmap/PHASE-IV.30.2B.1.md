# Phase IV.30.2B.1 — Pippin Discovers That Trees Aren't Rooms

Outdoor Scene Forge presentation refinement. Forest and Village retain their deterministic topology and authoritative LOS geometry, while presentation becomes environment-aware: outdoor terrain is calm rather than dungeon-tiled, tree and rock obstacles use organic decorative geometry, and Village buildings receive a building-specific roof/body vocabulary. Decorative geometry remains separate from rules geometry.
