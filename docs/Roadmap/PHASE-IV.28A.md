# Phase IV.28A — The Atlas Register

The Dungeon Master now receives a private **Keeper's Atlas** containing every
Scene retained by the current Table.

A new battlemap may be named and selected from the WordPress Media Library. It
is inspected server-side, persisted as a new square-grid Scene, and deliberately
left inactive. The currently active Scene is not disturbed until the Dungeon
Master explicitly chooses **Open Scene**.

The Atlas register itself is Dungeon-Master-only. Players continue to receive
only the active Scene. This privacy boundary is intentional groundwork for
IV.28B, where the Dungeon Master will be able to prepare future maps behind the
curtain without revealing them to connected adventurers.

Scene identity remains authoritative and scene-scoped systems remain untouched:
tokens, encounters, Fog of War, explored memory, grid calibration, doors, walls
and light stay attached to their own Scene when another map is opened.
