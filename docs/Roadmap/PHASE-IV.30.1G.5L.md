# Phase IV.30.1G.5L — Reconstructed Surface Propagation & Contour Re-certification

## The Floor Is Finally Allowed to Inform the Wall

G.5J and G.5K can recover playable surface hidden beneath dense illustration, but the Dungeon from Hell showed that this evidence could remain trapped inside the floor model: G.5I only emitted a newly inferred perimeter when its *own* closure pass had recovered the adjacent cell.

G.5L carries the provenance of G.5J reconstructed surface and G.5K illustrated-interior surface into the playable-region closure graph. A recovered cell may therefore ask the perimeter reader to reconsider its outer playable/non-playable transition.

This is deliberately local and conservative. A propagated cell must still belong to the certified playable region, have orthogonal closure support, meet the existing wall-body ink requirement, and survive the existing portal/threshold veto. Ordinary untouched floor is still owned by the normal Living Contour reader.

Most importantly, G.5J.1's monotonic contract remains authoritative. Re-certification is additive: existing certified contour objects are preserved first and newly re-certified perimeter evidence may only consume remaining review budget.

## Acceptance

- G.5J/G.5K recovered surface provenance reaches the G.5I closure/perimeter stage.
- Isolated illustrated flecks cannot request re-certification.
- Protected portals and thresholds remain open.
- Re-certified suggestions explicitly identify reconstructed-surface propagation evidence.
- Living Contour and Hybrid cannot become sparser than the pre-recovery baseline.
